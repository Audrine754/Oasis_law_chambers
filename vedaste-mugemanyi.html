About

Me Vedaste Mugemanyi is the founding and Managing Partner of Oasis Law Chambers. With more than 17 years in legal practice, he advises banks, businesses and individuals across banking, insurance, corporate, labour, competition and consumer law, and represents clients in dispute resolution, insolvency administration, and civil and criminal cases.

Before founding the firm, he served as Inspector of Judicial Policy, then as Mayor of Kabagari District and Deputy Mayor of Ruhango District in the Southern Province, bringing both legal and public-service experience to the advice he gives. He is also trained in mediation and in international law.
