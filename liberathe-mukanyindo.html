About

Me Liberathe MUKANYINDO is a Partner at Oasis Law Chambers, bringing four years of professional experience as a Legal Officer. She advises banks, businesses, and individuals on a broad range of legal matters, including banking, insurance, corporate law, labour law, competition law, and consumer protection. She has experience representing clients in dispute resolution, insolvency administration, and civil and criminal proceedings. In addition to her legal practice, she serves as a Mediator and Notary Public, offering professional legal guidance and support in resolving disputes and facilitating legal transactions.

With an academic background in both Law and Sociology, she combines legal expertise with a strong understanding of social and institutional dynamics, enabling her to provide well-rounded legal advice and representation.
