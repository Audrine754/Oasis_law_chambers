About

Me Alexandre Mugenzangabo is the founding and Senior Partner of Oasis Law Chambers, with more than 20 years of experience in legal practice. He advises on banking and financial law, business law and insolvency administration, and also on labour law, litigation, intellectual property, and the registration of companies and NGOs.

Before establishing the firm, he was Legal Adviser at a microfinance bank in Kigali, which gave him first-hand experience of how financial institutions work. He has been a registered advocate with the Kigali Bar Association since 9 June 2006.
