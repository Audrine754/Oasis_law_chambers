About

Me Jean Bosco Nubumwe is a Partner at Oasis Law Chambers with more than 17 years in legal practice. He advises and represents clients in criminal law, banking law, administrative law, competition and consumer law, labour law and dispute resolution.

Before joining the firm, he served as a Prosecutor and later as Executive Secretary of Gatenga Sector, giving him experience of both the justice system and local administration.
