About us

Who we are and what we stand for

Oasis Law Chambers is a dynamic, modern Rwandan law firm of legal consultants and advocates, committed to first-class legal services for businesses, organisations and individuals.

We practise employment and labour law, business and corporate law, insolvency administration, mediation, criminal matters, and environment and land law, among others. Our clients expect advice they can trust from lawyers whose independence and integrity are beyond question, and we work every day to meet and exceed that. To us, everything for our client matters.

The legal partner Rwanda’s financial institutions rely on. We are principal legal advisor to a number of banks and microfinance institutions, including:

  • Bank of Kigali Plc
  • BPR Bank Rwanda Plc
  • COPEDU Plc
  • and many more

Our vision

To be Rwanda’s most trusted law firm, known for independence, integrity and world-class legal service.

Our mission

To give businesses, organisations and individuals first-class legal advice and representation, responding promptly and guiding every client on the way forward.

Teamwork
